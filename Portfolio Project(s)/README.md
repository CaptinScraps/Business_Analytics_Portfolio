# 📂 Business Analyst Portfolio Projects

This folder contains links to my Business Analyst portfolio projects, case studies, and practical exercises completed as part of my transition into the Business Analysis profession.

The projects within this repository showcase my ability to apply Business Analysis principles to realistic business scenarios through requirements gathering, process analysis, stakeholder analysis, documentation, data interpretation, and solution recommendations.

## Panels Africa Analytics Project

The Panels Africa Analytics Project is a self-directed Business Analyst portfolio project based on a fictional business scenario that I developed using an existing case study as inspiration. While the structure was influenced by the SmartCart Business Analyst Portfolio Project, the business context, requirements, analysis, and deliverables were adapted to create a unique project focused on a different industry and business problem.

The objective of the project was to simulate the responsibilities of a Business Analyst by identifying business challenges, gathering and documenting requirements, analysing business processes, and proposing data-driven solutions to improve operational efficiency and decision-making.

The project demonstrates my ability to apply Business Analysis methodologies and produce professional documentation, including business requirements, stakeholder analysis, process modelling, and analytical insights within a realistic business environment.
